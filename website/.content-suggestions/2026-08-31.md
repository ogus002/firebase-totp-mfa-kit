**What changed and why:**

1. **Corrected the file-path description.** The old text hard-coded `app/(auth)/mfa-enroll` regardless of the `--area` flag value. The new wording reflects the actual behaviour: the destination path is derived from whatever argument is passed to `--area`.

2. **Added the env-var prerequisite block.** Developers who follow only this page will hit a Firebase initialisation error the moment they open the enroll route if `NEXT_PUBLIC_FIREBASE_*` vars are absent. Surfacing the three mandatory variables immediately after the install command closes that gap without duplicating content that belongs in `troubleshooting.md`.
